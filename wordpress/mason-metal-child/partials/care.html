Choosing your size

Measure a ring you already wear and compare its inner diameter. This sample offers illustrative sizes; the final store needs a verified size chart from the brand.

Caring for polished metal

Use a soft dry cloth and store pieces separately. Final care instructions should follow the actual metal, finish and manufacturer guidance.

Delivery & returns
